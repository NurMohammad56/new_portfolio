SolidSteps is a simple wellness app designed for approved users who want a private, minimal way to record daily step activity.

Users can manually enter step counts, view simple activity information, and access account-based features after login. SolidSteps is intentionally lightweight and easy to use, with a clean interface focused on basic daily step entry.

Some account-based support features are available only to users who have been pre-registered in the backend/admin system. Those features are not available through public self-registration and require approved account setup and user verification.

SolidSteps is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency dispatch, or emergency response services. Account-based support notification features, where available, are intended only to notify a designated support contact for follow-up outside the app.
