Zentrofix connects homeowners with skilled local service providers for home repair, maintenance, and improvement jobs.

Homeowners can post jobs, add details, set a location, upload images, review applicants, chat with tradespeople, track job progress, make payments, and leave feedback after the work is complete.

Service providers can discover nearby jobs, view job details, apply for work, manage current jobs, communicate with homeowners, and build their profile with reviews and job history.

With Zentrofix, managing home service work is easier from start to finish.

Key features:
- Post home service jobs with photos, category, location, and budget
- Find jobs near your area
- Chat between homeowners and service providers
- Track job progress
- Manage applications and job details
- View profiles, reviews, and service history
- In-app notifications for job updates
- Secure account, profile, and location management

Whether you need help at home or want to find local service work, Zentrofix helps you connect, communicate, and get the job done
